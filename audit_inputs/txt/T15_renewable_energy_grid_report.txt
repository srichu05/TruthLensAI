European Electrical Generation and Renewable Grid Integration Report

According to official quarterly grid telemetry released by European transmission system operators, solar and wind energy contributed over 30 percent of total European Union electrical generation during the second quarter of 2025. Utility-scale photovoltaic capacity additions in southern member states combined with strong offshore wind generation in the North Sea displaced fossil fuel dispatch.

Transmission grid operators managed transient peak generation periods using regional interconnections and battery storage installations. Wholesale day-ahead power prices exhibited localized negative pricing during midday solar peak hours, highlighting the ongoing requirement for flexible transmission infrastructure.
